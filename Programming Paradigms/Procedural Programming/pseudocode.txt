BEGIN

    GET instant coffee, water, milk, mug, teaspoon, kettle

    HEAT water in kettle until boiling

    ADD 2 teaspoons of instant coffee to mug

    POUR 200 ml of hot water into mug

    STIR until coffee dissolves

    ADD milk according to preference

    STIR coffee

    DO NOT ADD sugar

    SERVE coffee when it is safe to drink

END
